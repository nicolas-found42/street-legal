# Gameplay evidence notes A — 2026-10-22

## Scope and method

Primary visual evidence: downloaded YouTube footage in `re/out/gameplay-comparison-2026-10-22/videos/`, sampled into `frames/<video-id>/`. I inspected all 24 tiles in each of the six `contact-1.jpg` sheets listed below (144 sampled frames), plus `local/contact-sheet.png` (11 local captures). This is frame sampling, not complete viewing. Timestamps below come from the individual JPEG filenames, to hundredths of a second. The shots establish visible presentation and states; they do not establish sound, handling feel, frame rate, or the exact causal interaction between samples.

Titles, channels, dates, descriptions and declared editions below come from each corresponding downloaded `.info.json`. Uploader descriptions are claims about their setup, distinct from pixel observations. The apparent vehicle or UI modifications in several recordings mean this collection should not be treated as an unmodified original-game specification.

## Direct contrasts with the local game captures

Inspected `local/contact-sheet.png`, containing `01_title` through `11_test_drive`.

- The local garage is an intact exterior car over a dark checkerboard plane, beside a dense rectangular settings panel (`02_garage_overview`). Across the six reference sheets, workshop/car presentation occupies a larger share of the screen, often showing an inspection trench, tools, walls, doors and spatial context. Even the dark-background variant exposes individual internal assemblies. This is a substantial gap in workshop atmosphere and visible mechanical interaction.
- Local `03_parts_tyres` is a textual tire list with one-click fit prices; reference product catalogues show rendered individual components and parts inventory across the bottom of the 3D view. Local `04_mechanic` shows condition bars and bundled repair controls; reference stripped chassis and disconnected body/running-gear samples convey component-level assembly. These observations support a visible interaction-depth gap, but cannot alone prove every underlying capability of either game.
- Local `05_painter` has color swatches and `06_tuning` four direct sliders; reference clips show spray-can inventory, RGB paint sliders in one variant, component tuning modals, engine specifications and an RPM performance graph. The local UI already covers these broad purposes but with less mechanical context and fewer visual diagnostics.
- Local `09_race_hud` and `11_test_drive` show a smooth, flat-color car on a bounded track surrounded by repeated block trees and distant box buildings. Reference urban shots show connected streets, curbs, pavements, houses, shops/apartments, intersections, elevated roads, lamp posts, traffic and street gatherings. Reference night shots repeatedly show moon/stars, artificial light and headlight beams; the local captures show daytime only. Night support in local code was not assessed here.
- Local `08_events` explicitly lists race selections and reputation gates, while reference race participation is visibly situated in street gatherings with other cars and starter characters. Recreating that social/spatial staging is a more direct visual target than copying any one modded HUD.
- Local snapshots show a simplified intact body even during race/result capture; references visibly show damaged panels, missing panels, bare assemblies and a rolled car. No claim about local crash deformation follows solely from these shots; the local capture set simply does not demonstrate an equivalent visual damage state.

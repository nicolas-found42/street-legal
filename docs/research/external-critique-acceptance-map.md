# External critique traceability to exact-port acceptance

This appendix maps the 2026-10-22 [video-only comparison report](../../.scratch/report/comparison_report.md) to the existing [whole-game completion contract](../fidelity.md#whole-game-completion-contract) and [machine-readable obligation inventory](../../acceptance/matrix.json). It is a traceability aid, not another status inventory or a passing receipt. Current implementation status remains in [boot-status.md](../boot-status.md); computed acceptance remains the responsibility of `acceptance/run.sh`.

**Report `G001`–`G028` are finding identifiers. Canonical `G1`–`G11` are acceptance gates. They are different namespaces.** `C1`–`C9` below identify the report's proposed capture experiments. `P01`–`P30` and `S1`–`S13` retain their canonical meanings.

The report compared the retired `slr-core`/`slr-game` application in showcase A with sampled recordings. Its differences are not observations of the production exact port, and fixing that application cannot close these obligations. Applicable observations inform original-execution scenarios for unchanged stock scripts and assets. The architecture remains the native macOS arm64 port governed by ADRs [0004](../adr/0004-exact-function-level-port.md), [0005](../adr/0005-exact-sdat-saves.md), [0006](../adr/0006-completion-plan.md), [0007](../adr/0007-float-model-bugfixes-platform.md), [0008](../adr/0008-local-oracle-crates-natives.md), and [0009](../adr/0009-acceptance-per-tier.md). In particular, the later decisions control exact SDAT, all 353 natives, local oracle execution, bit-exact trusted physics, primary device-call equality, and removal of the approximation after the boot checkpoint.

## Common acceptance requirements for every applicable scenario

## Executable-validator blind spots and implementation dependencies

Inspection of `acceptance/check.py` shows registered live execution for S1, S10, S11 and the stock-checksum component of G1. S10 requires fresh S1 in the same invocation, retirement of the old approximation, a rebuilt replacement host replaying the complete S1 session, and repository checks. The other full obligations are unsupported by a complete registered validator at this inspection; links and historical receipts are supporting context only. This observation is about the checked dispatch source, not fresh execution of those live validators. Validators must be implemented at the same strength as the contract; adding a status or accepting an exit code would not repair the gap.

The executable entry point is `./acceptance/run.sh`, which dispatches to `acceptance/check.py:evaluate`. Its `--execute` choices are exactly `S1`, `S10`, `S11` and `G1` (`S10` automatically executes fresh `S1`); there is currently no command choice for a P row, device/scenario comparator, review receipt validator or S13 verifier. The separately retained [report-only inventory run](../../re/out/critique-map-audit-20261022T142918.677040Z/acceptance-table.json) exercised `--json --table`: it computed 51 unsupported, two not-run and one failed result, with `accepted: false`. Its successful shell exit means only that table reporting completed. New complete validators need checked-in dispatch here and fresh underlying execution; C1–C9 descriptions, evidence files, unit tests and matrix edits cannot register executable acceptance.

1. **Original replay and common provenance first.** Extend the permitted original oracle to execute feature sessions beyond completed MainMenu plus 300 idle frames. Bind candidate source bytes, executable, stock, original executable, oracle/shims, seed/clock/controls, tool versions and comparison policy to each result. Retain raw complete streams and first divergence, including failed attempts. This supports C1–C9 and G1–G3/G10/S1/S2/S11. The map does not claim the current boot frontier has been repaired.
2. **Scene/resource assembly and native devices are prerequisites to visible critique closure.** Production SCX/material/resource/worker ordering must feed actual render/window paths before matching workshop, cars, cameras, world, HUD or start/results screens. Add per-frame D3D9-equivalent logging and named-scene screenshot capture/review for S3/S5/S9, then attach them to C1–C6. Rendering a pool or a headless native log is insufficient.
3. **Stock-driven component/vehicle simulation must underlie transactions and driving.** Implement integrated slot/dependency graphs, tunable parts/effects, damage/wear and vehicle dynamics from original natives; compare with trusted float/control-word evidence before using C2/C3/C7/C9 as acceptance. Inventory all 24 models and all stock parts; a short representative drive cannot close S4/G4/G5/G11/P25/P26.
4. **Complete stock career transitions and persistence together.** Market, workshop, day/night races, pink slips, police and ROC must execute through unchanged scripts; transaction, time/rank/ownership and save/config consequences need complete scenario logs and G9/S8 cross-load. C1/C5/C6/C8 are connected ordinary workflows, not independent fixture pictures.
5. **Actual input and sound need separate executable and human evidence.** Add original-equivalent DirectInput/DirectSound capture/compare, ControlSet device exercise, synchronized live audio/video and review protocols for S6/S7/S12. Prepare exact human observations and outstanding receipts as soon as those paths are usable. A reviewer hears sound; an automated call comparator checks its stream. Neither substitutes for the other.
6. **Freeze only a complete rebuildable candidate for independent S13.** Preserve the committed revision or actual dirty patch/untracked source archive and every required input/tool/profile. A verifier that did not implement the candidate must freshly rebuild and rerun every matrix obligation and required repository/human checks. Findings in this appendix are traceability, not independent final verification.

The critique's nine experiments do **not** cover the entire contract. Explicit additional scenarios remain necessary for police chase/fines/consequences (P14), quick race (P16), every free-ride map including city2/ROC/test_track (P17–P20), ambient traffic and pedestrians (P21/P22), every day/night/sky/fog condition (P23), all parts/models (P25/P26), full sound/music and physical device sets (P28/P29), shipped Prime video and absent-intro behavior (P30/P01), complete format corpus/writer coverage (G2), every registered native including unreferenced ones (S2/G3), float trust/bit-exact transcendentals (G11), precise original-bug/CD-check exceptions (G10), retirement (S10), ledger regeneration (S11), and independent final verification (S13). Some are mentioned by the report, but none is exhausted by its sampled demonstrations.

The resulting decision is to use the report as a focused set of stock corroboration questions and observable regressions inside the canonical matrix. It neither authorizes weakening exactness nor importing the retired app's mechanics or modified-reference content into the production port.
